Pokémon Types in Japanese

All 18 types with hiragana, katakana, kanji and romaji

In the Japanese games, types that are native Japanese words are written in hiragana (ほのお, みず), and types borrowed from English are written in katakana (ドラゴン, ゴースト). The kanji column shows how the native words are written in everyday Japanese.
