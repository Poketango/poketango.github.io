💡 A big kana followed by a small ャ ュ ョ or ァ ィ ゥ ェ ォ is read together as one sound.

Japanese Pokémon names are written in katakana, the script used for foreign words and names. Learn the 5 characters above and you can read “グソクムシャ” yourself!

📖 Pokédex entry in English and Japanese

They live in sunken ships or in holes in the seabed. When Golisopod and Grapploct battle, the loser becomes the winner’s meal.

沈没船や海底の穴に生息。オトスパスと戦うと、負けた方が勝者の餌になる。

The Japanese text is a translation of the English entry, written with kanji, hiragana and katakana. Try to spot the katakana words!
